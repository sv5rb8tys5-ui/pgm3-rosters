- Every record retains its file's exact key set and expected types.
- No duplicate IDs; rating/potential bounds and potential >= rating pass.
- No negative or noninteger contract amounts/terms.
- Player growth arrays have 31 entries; staff growth arrays have 51; appearances have 9.

- All 32 teams retain exactly nine staff, one per modeled role.

- The 32 team standouts in PFF's midseason report were compared with existing ratings; the previous audit's standout upgrades were retained. No additional blanket OVR inflation was applied.
- Existing player IDs and draft metadata are unchanged.
- Staff assignments remain stable, but the merged follow-up audit adjusted ten further coaching ratings in staff.json.

## Limits
No in-app PGM3 import was available, so structural validation is not an in-game compatibility guarantee, especially for newly added players. A pre-audit backup branch is retained.
The legacy database includes simulated future prospects, fictional supplemental staff, modeled contracts, and an incomplete practice-squad pool. Injury/reserve status and traded future draft picks cannot be represented by these two files' existing schemas. Ages and other unchanged biography/contract fields have not all been independently reverified. This audit does not certify every historical detail or every NFL depth player as complete.
